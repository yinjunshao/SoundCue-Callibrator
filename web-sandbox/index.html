SoundCue ● Calibration Sandbox

Binaural HRTF listening test — measures how your ears + headphones map true sound direction to perceived direction. Output feeds SoundCue's perceptual azimuth correction.
3 unrecorded practice trials run first.
Samples are synthesized in-browser (no files needed).
Playback is loud — set to a comfortable level before starting.
How it works: put on headphones, keep your head still, look at the center crosshair. A sound plays somewhere around you in the void — turn your view with the mouse (the sound pans with your head, like in a game) until it sounds straight ahead, then click to confirm where you heard it. Distance is fixed at 3 m; azimuths are randomized across the full 360° including behind you. The mouse locks to the view (FPS-style). No network, no server — everything runs locally in this tab. Hearing nothing directional? Click "Test spatial audio" first — you should clearly hear claps move left → front → right. If they all sound identical, check that your headphones are the default output device and not muted, then try Edge or Chrome.
